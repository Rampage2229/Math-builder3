Social Mathematics

Statistics, probability, and data analysis for social sciences. Focus on real-world applications in psychology, sociology, economics, and public policy.

Track Overview

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply descriptive and inferential statistics to social science data
  • Design and analyze surveys with appropriate sampling methods
  • Interpret correlation and regression results in context
  • Evaluate ethical implications of data collection and analysis
  • Communicate statistical findings to non-technical audiences

Prerequisites

  • High school algebra
  • Basic familiarity with spreadsheets (Excel/Google Sheets)
