Descriptive Statistics

Measures of central tendency, dispersion, and data visualization for social science research.

Overview

Level: Introductory Duration: 3 weeks Lessons: 5

This unit introduces students to the fundamental tools for summarizing and visualizing data. Students learn to describe distributions using measures of center and spread, and to create meaningful visualizations that reveal patterns in social science data.

Lessons

  • Lesson 1.1: Data Types & Measurement Scales 2 days
  • Lesson 1.2: Measures of Central Tendency 3 days
  • Lesson 1.3: Measures of Dispersion 3 days
  • Lesson 1.4: Data Visualization 4 days
  • Lesson 1.5: Interpreting Distributions 3 days

Learning Outcomes

  • Distinguish between nominal, ordinal, interval, and ratio data
  • Calculate and interpret mean, median, and mode
  • Compute range, variance, and standard deviation
  • Create histograms, box plots, and bar charts
  • Identify skewness and outliers in distributions

Prerequisites

  • Basic arithmetic operations
  • Familiarity with variables and expressions
